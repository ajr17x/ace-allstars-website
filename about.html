Our Story

About Us

Since opening our doors, the programme has grown rapidly, attracting athletes of all ages and abilities from across Hertfordshire and the surrounding areas. Whether an athlete is taking their first steps into cheerleading or working towards international competition, we are committed to providing high-quality coaching in a supportive and positive environment.

In just a short time, our athletes have earned multiple national titles, international championships and prestigious bids to some of the sport's biggest events, including the European Summit and Allstar Worlds.

As we continue to grow, our commitment remains the same: to provide exceptional coaching, exciting opportunities and a community that athletes and families are proud to be part of.

Our Ethos

The safety and happiness of our athletes is central to everything we do. We want the gym to feel like a second home, a place where trust runs deep, both within our teams and between the club and the families who support them.
