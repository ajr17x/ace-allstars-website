About Us

Ace Allstars Cheer is a competitive all star cheerleading club based in Nazeing. In a short space of time we've become International U12 & U16 Champions and UCA National Champions, taking to some of the biggest stages in the sport.

But we're about more than trophies. Every session is about building strength, confidence and lifelong friendships — pushing every athlete to become the best version of themselves, on the mat and off it.

Whether you're throwing your first tumble or chasing your next title, there's a place for you at Ace Allstars.
