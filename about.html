About Us

Ace Allstars Cheer is a competitive all star cheerleading club based in Nazeing, welcoming athletes from across Hertfordshire and Essex — including Hoddesdon — and within easy reach of London. Now in our third season, we're proud to be 3x International Champions, UCA National Champions, and undefeated overseas, taking to some of the biggest stages in the sport, from Disney World to Paris.

The ACE Army

We want our athletes to feel like they are part of the ACE Army, with the knowledge that our gym will always be a safe space for them to express themselves.

Our Ethos

We know how vital the safety and happiness of our athletes is, and so our aim is for our gym to feel like a second home for our athletes and their families, building a level of trust both within our teams and between the club and our parents and guardians.
